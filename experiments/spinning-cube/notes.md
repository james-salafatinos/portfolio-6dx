# Spinning Cube

A minimal live-development experiment built with Jelly: a cube rotating about its X and Y axes, with a fixed Three.js axis helper at the world origin.

- Red is positive X, green is positive Y, and blue is positive Z.
- Drag to orbit the camera; scroll or pinch to zoom.
- The cube rotates while the world axes remain fixed.

Uses the shared Three.js modules and standard 6DX lifecycle. No data is collected or saved.
